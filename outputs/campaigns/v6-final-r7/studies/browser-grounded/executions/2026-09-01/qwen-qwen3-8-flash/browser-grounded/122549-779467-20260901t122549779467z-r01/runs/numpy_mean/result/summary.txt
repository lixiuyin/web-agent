**What numpy.mean computes**

Per the official NumPy reference documentation, `numpy.mean` **computes the arithmetic mean along the specified axis**.

- Signature (as shown on the page): `numpy.mean(a, axis=None, dtype=None, out=None, keepdims=<no value>, *, where=<no value>)`
- Behavior: "Returns the average of the array elements. The average is taken over the flattened array by default, otherwise over the specified axis. `float64` intermediate and return values are used for integer inputs."
- In other words, it is the plain (unweighted) arithmetic average of array elements — over the whole array when `axis=None`, or along the given axis/axes when `axis` is specified (with `keepdims` and `where` controlling dimension retention and element masking).
