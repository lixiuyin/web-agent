NumPy's official beginner guide is "NumPy: the absolute basics for beginners" in the NumPy user guide (NumPy v2.5 Manual), found via browser search and visited directly. Its central array object is the ndarray — the guide's opening section states "The ndarray is the core object of NumPy" (NumPy's homogeneous n-dimensional array, created e.g. with np.array). Exact URL: https://numpy.org/doc/stable/user/absolute_beginners.html
