IANA's page at https://www.iana.org/domains/reserved ("IANA-managed Reserved Domains") states: "Certain domains are set aside, and nominally registered to 'IANA', for specific policy or technical purposes." Under "Example domains," it explains that as described in RFC 2606 and RFC 6761, a number of domains such as example.com and example.org are maintained for documentation purposes; these domains may be used as illustrative examples in documents without prior coordination with IANA, and they are not available for registration. The page also references contractual limitations and ICANN-related IDN practices. Exact URL cited: https://www.iana.org/domains/reserved
