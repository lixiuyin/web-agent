IANA's official example-domains page is at https://www.iana.org/help/example-domains (title: "Example Domains"). Found via browser search (Bing and Yahoo! Japan results both listed it) and visited directly.

Purpose: As described in RFC 2606 and RFC 6761, IANA maintains a number of domains such as example.com and example.org for documentation purposes. These domains may be used as illustrative examples in documents without prior coordination with IANA, and they are not available for registration or transfer. IANA also provides a best-effort web service on the example domain hosts to give basic information about the purpose of the domain (not designed to support end-user traffic). The page is part of IANA's broader "IANA-managed Reserved Domains" (https://www.iana.org/domains/reserved) documentation.
